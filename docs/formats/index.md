rather than as bytes.

On a Mac, other pictures — `heic`, `avif`, `psd`, `icns`, camera raw — are drawn by the system's
own decoder; everywhere the app draws `png`, `jpeg`, `gif`, `webp`, `tiff`, `bmp` and `ico`
itself. `stl`, `usdz`, `rar`, `xls`, `ods` and `odp` have no reader here, and open in hex.

Every one of these parsers runs on your machine. Nothing is sent anywhere to be rendered — see
[your data stays here](/privacy).
