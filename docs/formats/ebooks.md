# Ebooks

`epub` `mobi` `prc` `azw` `azw3` `fb2` `fbz` `cbz` `cbt` `cbr`

<AppDemo open="folder/demo/ebook/the-lighthouse-keeper.epub" image="/screenshot/ebook.png" alt="An epub opened as a book, showing its cover" />

Open an `.epub`, `.mobi`, `.azw3`, `.fb2` or a `.cbz` comic and it opens as a book: a cover, then a
chapter a page, and the side arrows or the arrow keys to turn it.

The text reflows to a reading width, so a chapter reads the same whether the file came from a bucket
or from a zip on your disk — no Kindle, no Calibre, no converting it first.

A comic is its page scans, one a page: a `.cbz` is a zip of them and a `.cbt` a tar. A `.cbr` is
RAR, which is not read, unless it is really a zip under that name.
