## Today

`year=2026/month=09/day=11` read by a console as three thousand folders to click through, and a
guess at which ones the report actually used.

## Here

Hive partitions and bare date prefixes are recognised as partitioning, so the folder answers as one
table. Σ prices the whole prefix while you are there.
