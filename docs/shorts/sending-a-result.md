## Today

Export to csv, open it, delete the columns by hand, hope nothing was missed, attach it to an email
that now lives in two inboxes forever.

## Here

Share the tabular object, decide column by column — hash, mask, FPE or drop — and the preview is the
server's own answer, so what you see is what they get. The link expires in 1D to 1Y, or burns on
first read. FPE keeps ids joinable, so the recipient can still count and group.
