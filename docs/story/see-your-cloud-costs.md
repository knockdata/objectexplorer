## Today

A billing export, a query over it, and a per-bucket total that names no prefix and no cause.

## Here

COST/MO beside SIZE on every row, and Σ to price a whole prefix. Open Optimize and the causes are
itemised: a prefix that should be colder, a multi-region bucket that need not be, noncurrent versions
nobody knew existed, multipart uploads abandoned two years ago. Each row gives the monthly saving,
what acting costs, and the command that does it.
