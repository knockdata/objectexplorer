The quiet one. Nobody files a ticket about it, and it is on the bill every month.

## Today

Every look is a fresh `GET`, and a 900 MB file read thirty-six times is 32 GB of egress for one
afternoon of curiosity. Multiply by a team and a quarter.

## Here

The object comes down once per machine and is served from the local cache after that, so the second
look is instant and free. Show Cache Savings puts a number on it: reads, hits, bytes not pulled, and
the egress not paid for.
