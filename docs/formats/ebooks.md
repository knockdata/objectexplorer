# Ebooks

`epub` `kepub` `mobi` `prc` `azw` `azw3` `fb2` `fbz` `pdb` `chm` `lit` `lrf` `htmlz` `txtz`, and
comics as `cbz` `cbr` `cb7` `cbt`

<AppDemo open="folder/demo/ebook/the-lighthouse-keeper.epub" image="/screenshot/ebook.png" alt="An epub opened as a book, showing its cover" />

Open an `.epub`, `.mobi`, `.azw3`, `.fb2` or a `.cbz` comic and it opens as a book: a cover, then a
chapter a page, and the side arrows or the arrow keys and `PageUp` `PageDown` to turn it. The line
under the page says where you are — `1 of 4 · Cover`.

The text reflows to a reading width, so a chapter reads the same whether the file came from a bucket
or from a zip on your disk — no Kindle, no Calibre, no converting it first. **A−** and **A+** make
the text smaller or larger, and the size is kept for the next book. **Contents** lists the chapters
by their titles down the side; click one to go to it.

A Windows help file (`.chm`) and a Microsoft Reader `.lit` open as a book of their pages. A Kindle
Print Replica `.azw4` is a PDF inside, and opens in [the PDF reader](/formats/pdf).

A comic is its page scans, one a page: a `.cbz` is a zip of them, a `.cbr` RAR, a `.cb7` 7-Zip and a
`.cbt` a tar — whichever the first bytes say it is.
