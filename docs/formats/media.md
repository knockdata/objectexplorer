# Audio and video

`mp3` `wav` `m4a` `aiff` `bwf` `3gpp`, and video played in place

Audio opens as a waveform and a spectrogram, so a recording is something you can look at before you
listen to it — where the silence is, where it clips, where the noise floor sits.

Video plays in place, streamed by range requests from the object rather than downloaded whole first.
