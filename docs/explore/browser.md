# The Browser

Web pages in the desktop window, a tab each, beside your files: read a page next to the data it is
about, and open what it downloads in the app. On macOS and Windows; the browser page at
objectexplorer.com/app and the Linux window have no Browser.

**Browser**, the globe in the activity bar, opens it. The side bar lists **Open Tabs**, and two
more parts: [Heroes](/explore/heroes), the people you follow, and **Bookmarks**. **+** in its header
opens a new tab, as does **Browser: New Tab** in the command palette.

## A tab

Each tab has **Back**, **Forward**, **Reload** (**Stop** while it loads) and an address bar. An
address is opened as it is; words are searched with DuckDuckGo. A new tab opens the home page,
Google's unless **Settings → Browser** names another.

The tabs you leave open are there at the next start. Every tab shares one session: signed in to a
site in one tab, you are signed in to it in all of them, and the sign-in is kept when the app starts
again. A tab's page stays as it is while another tab or another activity shows.

## Bookmarks

The star at the end of the address bar keeps the page shown under a name you choose; **Bookmarks**
in the side bar lists them by name, and a click opens one in a new tab. **Edit Bookmark** and
**Remove Bookmark** show over its name.

## Downloads

A file a page downloads is saved in your Downloads folder; the bar over the page says so and opens
it in a tab of the app, as any file is opened. **Download and Open** in a link's right-click menu
does both at once.

## Words on a page

Select a word and right-click: **Translate** shows it in every language you learn, and **Mark New
Words** in the toolbar takes the page's hardest words as flash cards — see
[Translate and QLingo](/agents/translate). Both are there once an agent is set up in
**Settings → Ask**.

The pages you open are loaded from their sites, as in any browser — see
[your data stays here](/privacy#what-does-leave-and-when).
