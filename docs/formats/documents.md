# Documents and presentations

`pdf` `docx` `dotx` `pptx` `potx` `ppsx` `ppt` `pot` `pps` `key` `xlsx` `xltx` `evernote`

<img src="/screenshot/presentation.png" alt="A pptx rendered slide by slide">

A PDF opens in [the PDF reader](/formats/pdf), with selectable text, find and notes. A Word file renders as its text with its structure. A presentation
renders slide by slide — including the old binary `.ppt` and Keynote's `.key`.

Because the office formats are zips, the same objects can also be
[browsed as folders](/explore/archives): open the `.pptx` as an archive and the slide XML, the media
and the theme are entries you can open one at a time.
